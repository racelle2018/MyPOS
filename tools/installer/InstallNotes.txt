RetailForma installation and updates

Close RetailForma and verify a backup before updating.

Administrator permission is required to install in C:\RetailForma.

The protected data service stores company data in %PROGRAMDATA%\RetailForma\Data.
Local encrypted backup ZIPs remain in %APPDATA%\RetailForma\backups.

Updates and uninstall retain company data, recovery material and backups.
Use Technician Setup to create a new company or explicitly import a verified encrypted backup.
